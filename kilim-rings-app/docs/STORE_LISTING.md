# App Store metni (taslak)

Uygulama adı "Kilim Rings" App Store'da başka bir uygulamayla çakışabilir; App Store Connect'te kayıt sırasında kontrol edilir.
Çakışırsa "Kilim Rings: Ring Puzzle" gibi bir ad kullan.

## English (U.S.)
- **Name:** Kilim Rings
- **Subtitle (≤30):** Calm ring-turning puzzle
- **Promotional text:** 120 handmade-style levels. No timer, no lives, no ads.
- **Keywords (≤100):** puzzle,rings,rotate,pattern,kilim,relaxing,offline,brain,logic,one hand,tile
- **Description:**
  Turn the rings until the pattern falls into place.

  Kilim Rings is a calm, one-thumb puzzle inspired by Anatolian kilim patterns. Drag a ring, match the target pattern, and collect stars for solving it in few moves.

  • 120 levels in four chapters: Kilim, Tile, Marbling, Mosaic
  • Bridges join rings so several turn together; locked rings add a twist
  • Unlimited hints and undo, no timer, no lives, no ads
  • Colour-blind friendly: every colour also has its own shape
  • Works offline. No account, no data collection
  • Turkish and English
